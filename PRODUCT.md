# Product

## Register
Product (B2B SaaS / ERP app shell, dense data tables, operations & finance consoles, customer billing portal).

## Users & Purpose
- **Primary Users:**
  - **Administrators & Operations Staff:** Managing customers, product catalogs, subscription lifecycles, and approving customer requests.
  - **Finance Staff:** Invoices, recording & reconciling payments, resolving billing run failures, revenue reporting.
  - **Customers:** Managing recurring subscriptions, viewing and downloading invoices, submitting change requests.
- **Workflow & Core Task:** High-integrity financial management where every number is backed by immutable audit trails, idempotent transactions, and strict access controls.

## Brand & Personality
- **Voice & Aesthetic:** Editorial Tech Journal on Warm Parchment (inspired by Monad / Stripe / Linear).
- **Core Tones:** Restrained, precise, technical-literary authority.
- **Typographic Philosophy:** Untitled Serif (or Georgia/Times fallback) at weight 400 for authoritative editorial headings; ABC Diatype Mono (or JetBrains/Space Mono) for all functional UI, tables, buttons, and badges.
- **Anti-References:**
  - No generic corporate blue dashboards with harsh gradients or heavy drop shadows.
  - No pure-white (#ffffff) glare or default cold slate backgrounds.
  - No decorative AI glassmorphism, side-stripe borders, or eyebrow labels over headings.
  - No bold screaming serifs — headings whisper with stroke contrast and tight letter-spacing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Anchored to **WCAG AA** standards.
- High-contrast text readability (Graphite `#4e4d4d` and Off-Black `#242424` on Parchment `#f6f3f1` reaching ≥ 4.5:1).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance across all transitions.
- Fully accessible keyboard navigation and focus states for dense tabular forms.
